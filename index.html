    Questions people ask

    Which Florida beach should I pick?

    There is no single best beach. Use the picker for the constraint that would ruin the day: region, dogs, lifeguards, a wheelchair or mat, shelling, or a kids playground or guarded swim. Then open the beach page and the agency link.

    Do you post today's red tide or bacteria results?

    No. FWC posts the current status and a daily sample map. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results. This site links those pages and does not freeze a cell count.

    Where do the parking, dog, and access facts come from?

    From the county or city park page, or the Florida State Parks page, linked on each beach. If a fee, an hour, or a fill time is not on that page, it is not on ours.

    Which FL Beach helps you pick a Florida beach from facts published by county and city parks, Florida State Parks, the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, and the Florida Department of Health. We do not post live water conditions. Read the managing agency page before you go.
