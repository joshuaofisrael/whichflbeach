About Which FL Beach

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Which FL Beach is an independent guide to choosing a Florida beach. The question on every page is practical: parking, shells, red tide, step free access, dogs, lifeguards, restrooms, and who the beach suits.

What we cover

73 beaches on the Gulf Coast, the Atlantic coast, the Panhandle and the Keys, with extra depth on Siesta Key and Sarasota County. We add a beach when we can cite a county, city, or Florida State Parks page.

How we work

We read the managing agency page, write the summary ourselves, and link it. If a fee, an hour, or a fill time is not on that page, it is not on ours. Live red tide and bacteria results stay on FWC and the Florida Department of Health. See sources and methods.

Independence

Which FL Beach is not a park agency and is not paid by one. Place names are used only to identify the beaches.

See a stale fact after a park changes its page? Write to us.

Which FL Beach helps you pick a Florida beach from facts published by county and city parks, Florida State Parks, the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, and the Florida Department of Health. We do not post live water conditions. Read the managing agency page before you go.
