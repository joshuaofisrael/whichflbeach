Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which FL Beach (whichflbeach.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

When the public site is launched, pages may include Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. That product does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. The preview build of this site does not include the analytics script.

Contact

If you email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com, we use your message and your address to reply. We keep them only as long as we need for that reply and for a record of a correction.

Advertising

The site shows no ads today. If we add ads later, for example through Google AdSense, vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You would be able to opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings and at aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, we will ask for consent before advertising cookies are set. We will update this policy before any ads go live.

Cookies

Which FL Beach itself sets no cookies. The site works if your browser blocks them.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold. Email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com. Residents of some states and countries may have additional rights, which we will honor.

Children

The site is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new date.
