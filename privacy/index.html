Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026. This policy explains how Which FL Beach (whichflbeach.com), a trade name of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC, a Florida limited liability company, handles information.

Analytics

The public site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics 4.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. It does not use cookies and does not track you across sites. The launch build includes that beacon on every page. The preview build does not include it.

Google Analytics 4 measures page visits for this site. The launch build loads the Google tag (gtag) only after you accept the cookie banner. Until then, Google Consent Mode v2 defaults are denied for ad storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and analytics storage. If you accept, analytics storage is set to granted and the tag loads for our GA4 property. Ad storage stays denied until advertising consent is collected. If you reject, the tag is not loaded. Use the Cookie settings link in the footer to change your choice. That choice is stored in local storage in your browser. The preview build does not include Google Analytics 4.

Contact

If you email joshuaofisrael@gmail.com, we use your message and your address to reply. We keep them only as long as we need for that reply and for a record of a correction.

Advertising

Which FL Beach uses Google AdSense account ca-pub-5681559774142917. The site includes the AdSense script and a google-adsense-account meta tag so Google can verify the property. We have not placed ad units on pages yet, so you should not see display ads today.

Our Consent Mode defaults keep ad storage denied until that consent step is added. Publisher details also appear in the site root ads.txt file.

An advertiser does not choose beach facts. See the advertising disclosure.

Cookies

Which FL Beach itself sets no cookies. Google Analytics 4 may set cookies only after you accept. Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies. When AdSense ad units are active and consent is granted, Google may set advertising cookies. The site works if your browser blocks cookies.
