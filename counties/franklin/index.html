Franklin County beaches

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Franklin County has 3 beaches in this guide. Each page uses that county's park page or the Florida State Parks page for the park, plus FWC and the Florida Department of Health for shells, red tide and bacteria.

  • St. George Island State Park

    St. George Island State Park is the Franklin County Gulf park with 9 miles of beach in the description, mats at named accesses, and several kinds of beach chair.

  • Bald Point Sunrise Beach

    Sunrise Beach is one of two named Bald Point beaches. The page gives it a mat, showers, and a shared beach wheelchair.

  • Bald Point North End Beach

    North End Beach is the other Bald Point swim, with a mat and a fishing pier the page also fits with a mat.

Where do the Franklin County facts come from?

From the county or city park page, or the Florida State Parks experiences page, linked on each beach. Live red tide and bacteria results stay on FWC and the Florida Department of Health.
