Sarasota County beaches

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 21 Nov 2026

Sarasota County says select beach parking is free. Siesta Beach states over 950 free spaces. Several accesses are titled as no parking.

The beaches directory checked 21 Nov 2026 says parking is available at select Sarasota County beaches and parks for free, on a first-come, first-served basis. It does not print a dollar amount. It also does not say every shoreline has a lot. The titles that take the lot away are North Lido (no parking), Palmer Point (no parking, watercraft access only), Siesta Key accesses 3, 3B, 10, 11, and 13 (no parking, pedestrian only), and Turtle Beach Campground (no beach parking). Access 2 is titled as one accessible space and no other parking. This guide does not give Access 2 its own page. Access 11 is the odd title: no parking, and a lifeguard still on the amenity list.

The hours page, which that site dates 25 Feb 2025 and which we opened 21 Nov 2026, says the main Siesta Beach lot is free and open year round from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m., with room for a recreational vehicle. It names accesses 3, 3B, 10, 11, and 13 as open 24 hours with no parking. It also says there is no overnight parking in county beach lots, and that lot hours are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. unless a sign says otherwise. The Siesta destination page, opened the same day, says there are over 950 free spaces. That is the largest count the county prints in this set. The same two county pages disagree on the access mat (504 feet on the directory, 454 feet on the destination page) and on the lifeguard clock (4:45 p.m. on the directory, 5 p.m. on the destination page). The free lot is the part they agree on.

Caspersen is not a free lot. The facility page checked 20 Nov 2026 said the Harbor Drive vehicle lot was closed after the 2024 storm washout, with entry on foot or by bicycle from South Brohard. We did not reopen that facility page on 21 Nov. The directory listing does not repeat the closure in its title.

These county pages do not describe meters. The paid pattern in this guide is Pinellas County and the state parks, written out on the free parking guide.

Questions people ask

Is Sarasota County beach parking free?

The directory checked 21 Nov 2026 says parking at select beaches and parks is free and first come, first served. It does not print a dollar amount. Titles that say no parking are exceptions. Siesta Beach states over 950 free spaces.

Which Sarasota beaches have no parking?

The directory titles name North Lido, Palmer Point, Siesta accesses 3, 3B, 10, 11, and 13, and Turtle Beach Campground. Access 2 is limited to one accessible space. Caspersen's vehicle lot was closed on the facility page checked 20 Nov 2026.

Do the county pages describe meters?

No. The directory and the hours page describe free select lots, lot hours, and named accesses with no parking. They do not describe meters.
