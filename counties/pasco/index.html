Pasco County beaches

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 21 Nov 2026

Anclote Key is the Pasco beach in this guide. You arrive by boat or ferry. The park page prints no car lot and no lifeguard on duty.

Pasco's entry in this guide is one island preserve, not a row of drive-up lots. Anclote Key Preserve State Park was rechecked on 21 Nov 2026 against the Florida State Parks experiences page. The boating section says you can reach the islands by boat or by ferry from Tarpon Springs or New Port Richey. It does not name the ferry, print a fare, or describe a car lot on the island. A search for free parking ends at the water. If a ferry charges, that price is not on the park page, so it is not here.

The swimming section on that same check says there are no lifeguards on duty and that swimming is at your own risk. The beach section says lifeguards are not provided and tells visitors to read the warning flag signs. This page does not copy the color list. A flag drawing on the park site is not a forecast we repeat.

Dogs are split in one sentence pair. Pets are not permitted on Anclote Key. Pets are permitted only on North Anclote Bar, on a six foot handheld leash. Shell collecting is listed as a favorite activity, and collecting live shellfish is prohibited.

An 1,800 foot section of Three Rooker beach is closed from May 1 to August 1 for nesting shorebirds. The boating section says the park covers nearly 12,000 acres of gulf waters. The fishing section says anglers report sea trout, redfish, snook, and sharks. That is the park's note about what people catch. It is not a promise for the day you go.

The historic site section says the lighthouse is 110 feet and was built in 1887, and that it is open to the public only during special events. Primitive camping is permitted on the very north end of Anclote Key. The page says there is no water and no other amenities there, so you bring what you need and pack it out. A compostable toilet serves that camping area. A second compostable toilet sits a short distance from the lighthouse compound. The page does not describe running water.

Questions people ask

Is there free parking at Anclote Key?

No car lot is described. The experiences page checked 21 Nov 2026 says you arrive by boat or by ferry from Tarpon Springs or New Port Richey, and it does not print a fare or a mainland lot fee.

Can I bring a dog to Anclote Key?

Pets are not permitted on Anclote Key. They are permitted only on North Anclote Bar, on a six foot handheld leash.

Are there lifeguards at Anclote Key?

The swimming section says there are no lifeguards on duty and that swimming is at your own risk. The beach section says lifeguards are not provided.

Is the lighthouse open every day?

The page says the 110 foot lighthouse was built in 1887 and is open to the public only during special events.
