Image policy

Last updated 19 Nov 2026.

Graphics

The logo and the social image are original graphics we drew for this site. They are not photographs of a beach, and they are not copies of a park map or logo.

Photos

We do not copy park photography. When a guide uses a news style thumbnail, the image links here and carries a credit. Current thumbnails are hosted on this site under /assets/images/guides/, including /assets/images/guides/shore-snorkeling-florida.jpg and /assets/images/guides/beach-warning-flag.png.

AI generated images

Guide thumbnails that are AI generated carry a visible credit that says AI generated image, link to this page, do not depict a real identifiable person, and are not presented as documentary photos of a named park. Until a guide has its own news photo, related read cards may use the site social graphic instead.

Names

We do not display agency logos. Names identify places.

If you believe a graphic uses your work, see the copyright policy or contact us.

Which FL Beach helps you pick a Florida beach from facts published by county and city parks, Florida State Parks, the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, and the Florida Department of Health. We do not post live water conditions. Read the managing agency page before you go.
