Beaches that suit kids

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

How this list is made. A beach is here when the agency page lists a playground, staffed lifeguards, or a protected spot it describes for children. Calm looking water is not a promise.

Lifeguard hours end. Flags change. Read the beach page for what is actually staffed, and the red tide guide the morning you go. A playground on the amenity list is not a lifeguard. Anastasia lists a playground in the beach access area and staffs a designated swim only from Memorial Day through Labor Day. Caspersen is not on this list: the county says the playground is closed, and a lifeguard is not in the amenity list.

Beaches

  • Siesta Beach. Siesta Beach is the Sarasota County park with the large free lot, a published lifeguard window, a free beach wheelchair, and an access mat.
  • Lido Beach. Lido Beach is the county beach on Lido Key with a lifeguard, a concession, a pool and restrooms on the directory list.
  • Ted Sperling Park at South Lido. Ted Sperling Park at South Lido is the county park at the south end of Lido Key. The directory lists a playground, restrooms, picnicking and an unpaved trail, and it does not list swimming or a lifeguard.
  • Nokomis Beach. Nokomis Beach is a guarded county beach with a playground, a concession, a fishing pier, a boat ramp and sand volleyball on the directory list.
  • North Jetty Park. North Jetty Park is a guarded county beach at the south end of Casey Key, with a playground, a concession and a kayak launch on the directory list.
  • Venice Beach. Venice Beach is a guarded county beach with a concession, sand volleyball and restrooms. The directory does not mention shark teeth or shelling.
  • Manasota Beach. Manasota Beach is a guarded county beach on Manasota Key with boat ramps for motorized and paddle craft, fishing and restrooms.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 11. Siesta Key Beach Access 11 is a no parking pedestrian access that still lists a lifeguard. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.
  • Coquina Beach. Coquina Beach is a Manatee County beach on Anna Maria with a lifeguard station, beach wheelchairs, a playground, and an introduction that talks about the sun setting over the water.
  • Manatee Public Beach. Manatee Public Beach is the Holmes Beach county park with a published all year lifeguard schedule and beach wheelchairs on the amenity list.
  • Cortez Beach. Cortez Beach is the third Manatee County beach the lifeguard page names. That page is specific about guards and dogs, and silent about the lot and the wheelchair.
  • Fort De Soto Park. Fort De Soto is the Pinellas County park with miles of Gulf beach, two swim centers, and the only county dog beach.
  • Sand Key Park. Sand Key is the Clearwater county beach with paid parking, a dog park that is not the beach, and seasonal lifeguards named in a county notice.
  • Honeymoon Island State Park. Honeymoon Island is the Pinellas state park with a named pet beach, shelling on the north spit, and free beach wheelchairs.
  • Henderson Beach State Park. Henderson Beach is the Destin state park with 422 day use spaces, a beach wheelchair, and a page that mentions sunset.
  • Big Lagoon State Park. Big Lagoon is the Pensacola state park the page is careful to call an Intracoastal beach, not a Gulf beach.
  • St. Andrews State Park. St. Andrews is the Panama City state park with more than a mile and a half of beach, a jetty, and a ferry onward to Shell Island.
  • St. George Island State Park. St. George Island State Park is the Franklin County Gulf park with 9 miles of beach in the description, mats at named accesses, and several kinds of beach chair.
  • Anastasia State Park. Anastasia State Park has four miles of beach, a fee that covers 350 spaces, a Mobi-Mat, and lifeguards only from Memorial Day through Labor Day.
  • Sebastian Inlet State Park. Sebastian Inlet is the Atlantic park with a protected cove the page recommends for children, plus surf, beachcombing, and no lifeguards.
  • John D. MacArthur Beach State Park. MacArthur Beach is the Palm Beach County state park with nearly two miles of Atlantic beach, three free wheelchairs, and shelling in the park's own list.
  • Hugh Taylor Birch State Park. Hugh Taylor Birch is the Fort Lauderdale state park that opens onto the city beach, with 252 spaces and a beach wheelchair at the ranger station.
  • Crandon Park Beach. Crandon Park is the Key Biscayne county beach with a published parking price, a lot under 3,000 spaces, and beach wheelchairs.

Questions people ask

Are these beaches lifeguarded all day?

Only if that beach page says so. Some parks staff a seasonal window. Others say lifeguards are not provided.

Is a playground the same as a guarded swim?

No. A playground is on land. Read the lifeguard line before anyone goes in.
