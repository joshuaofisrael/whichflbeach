Where the sun sets over the water

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

How to use this page. On the Gulf, the open water is to the west, so a clear evening puts the sun over the water. Atlantic beaches look toward sunrise instead. This is geography, not a score. Weather and buildings still block the view.

Park pages that mention sunset

  • Coquina Beach. Coquina Beach is a Manatee County beach on Anna Maria with a lifeguard station, beach wheelchairs, a playground, and an introduction that talks about the sun setting over the water.
  • Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park. Delnor Wiggins is the Collier state park the page credits with some of the best Gulf shelling, four beach lots, and both manual and electric beach wheelchairs.
  • Henderson Beach State Park. Henderson Beach is the Destin state park with 422 day use spaces, a beach wheelchair, and a page that mentions sunset.

Other Gulf beaches in this guide

These also face the Gulf. Their park pages may not discuss sunset. The direction is the same.

  • Siesta Beach. Siesta Beach is the Sarasota County park with the large free lot, a published lifeguard window, a free beach wheelchair and a 454 foot access mat.
  • Turtle Beach. Turtle Beach is the staffed county park at the south end of Siesta Key, with a boat ramp, a fishing pier and a playground, and no lifeguard in the directory list.
  • Turtle Beach Campground. Turtle Beach Campground is the county campground on Siesta Key. The directory title says it has no beach parking.
  • Lido Beach. Lido Beach is the county beach on Lido Key with a lifeguard, a concession, a pool and restrooms on the directory list.
  • North Lido Beach. North Lido Beach is the county access titled as having no parking. The directory lists beach access, birding, picnicking, an unpaved trail and wildlife viewing.
  • Ted Sperling Park at South Lido. Ted Sperling Park at South Lido is the county park at the south end of Lido Key. The directory lists a playground, restrooms, picnicking and an unpaved trail, and it does not list swimming or a lifeguard.
  • Nokomis Beach. Nokomis Beach is a guarded county beach with a playground, a concession, a fishing pier, a boat ramp and sand volleyball on the directory list.
  • North Jetty Park. North Jetty Park is a guarded county beach at the south end of Casey Key, with a playground, a concession and a kayak launch on the directory list.
  • Venice Beach. Venice Beach is a guarded county beach with a concession, sand volleyball and restrooms. The directory does not mention shark teeth or shelling.
  • Manasota Beach. Manasota Beach is a guarded county beach on Manasota Key with boat ramps for motorized and paddle craft, fishing and restrooms.
  • Caspersen Beach. Caspersen Beach is the park the county introduction calls a secluded offering. The directory lists swimming, fishing, a kayak launch, fitness equipment and an unpaved trail, and it does not list a lifeguard or a restroom.
  • Blind Pass Beach. Blind Pass Beach is a county park on Manasota Key with restrooms, swimming, fishing, a kayak launch and picnic shelters. A lifeguard is not in the list.
  • Palmer Point Beach. Palmer Point is the county beach at the south end of Siesta Key that the directory limits to watercraft access. It lists swimming, fishing, a kayak launch and an unpaved trail.
  • Service Club Park. Service Club Park is a county beach in Venice with restrooms, sand volleyball, grills and picnic shelters. A lifeguard is not in the list.
  • Maxine Barritt Park. Maxine Barritt Park is one of two Sarasota County beach listings marked dog friendly with a leash required. It also lists swimming, fishing, restrooms and a paved trail.
  • North Brohard Park. North Brohard Park is a Venice beach listing with a fishing pier, a bait shop, a concession and restrooms. A lifeguard is not in the list.
  • South Brohard Park. South Brohard Park is a short county listing: beach access, birding, fishing, a restroom, swimming and wildlife viewing. No lifeguard is listed.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 1. Siesta Key Beach Access 1 is the island access the county marks dog friendly with a leash required. The directory also lists swimming, birding and wildlife viewing, and it does not list a lifeguard or a restroom.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 3. Siesta Key Beach Access 3 is one of the pedestrian accesses the county hours page says are open all hours and have no parking.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 3B. Siesta Key Beach Access 3B is named with Access 3 on the county hours page as a 24 hour pedestrian access with no parking.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 4. Siesta Key Beach Access 4 is a numbered neighborhood access. The directory gives it the short amenity list and does not list a lifeguard or a restroom.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 5. Siesta Key Beach Access 5 is a separate county access with the short amenity list. It is not the main Siesta Beach pavilion.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 7. Siesta Key Beach Access 7 is another numbered access with beach, birding, swimming, an unpaved trail and wildlife viewing on the directory list.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 8. Siesta Key Beach Access 8 carries the short directory list and is not the lifeguard access.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 9. Siesta Key Beach Access 9 is the last of the short list accesses before the pedestrian only points the hours page names.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 10. Siesta Key Beach Access 10 is titled as pedestrian access with no parking. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 11. Siesta Key Beach Access 11 is a no parking pedestrian access that still lists a lifeguard. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 12. Siesta Key Beach Access 12 is a numbered access the directory does not mark as no parking. The amenity list is still short: beach, birding, swimming, an unpaved trail and wildlife viewing.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 13. Siesta Key Beach Access 13 is a no parking pedestrian access at the south end of the public list. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.
  • Manatee Public Beach. Manatee Public Beach is the Holmes Beach county park with a published all year lifeguard schedule and beach wheelchairs on the amenity list.
  • Cortez Beach. Cortez Beach is the third Manatee County beach the lifeguard page names. That page is specific about guards and dogs, and silent about the lot and the wheelchair.
  • Fort De Soto Park. Fort De Soto is the Pinellas County park with miles of Gulf beach, two swim centers, and the only county dog beach.
  • Sand Key Park. Sand Key is the Clearwater county beach with paid parking, a dog park that is not the beach, and seasonal lifeguards named in a county notice.
  • Honeymoon Island State Park. Honeymoon Island is the Pinellas state park with a named pet beach, shelling on the north spit, and free beach wheelchairs.
  • Caladesi Island State Park. Caladesi is the island park reached by ferry from Honeymoon Island. The page promotes shelling and bans live shells and pets on the beach.
  • Anclote Key Preserve State Park. Anclote Key is a boat or ferry preserve in Pasco. Shells are mentioned, live shellfish are not allowed, and dogs are limited to North Anclote Bar.
  • Egmont Key State Park. Egmont Key is a boat only island in Tampa Bay with shelling, no restrooms, and a south end the page closes as a shorebird refuge.
  • Stump Pass Beach State Park. Stump Pass is the Charlotte County state park beach the page ties to winter shelling, with free wheelchairs and no lifeguards.
  • Don Pedro Island State Park. Don Pedro is a boat only Gulf beach in Charlotte County. The drive up land base is not the swim.
  • Lovers Key State Park. Lovers Key is the Lee County state park that calls its own beach some of the best shelling on that coast, and that warns the lot fills on busy weekends.
  • Gasparilla Island State Park. Gasparilla Island State Park is the Boca Grande Gulf beach where the page highlights winter shelling and several named lots.
  • Cayo Costa State Park. Cayo Costa is a boat access Lee County island the park page recommends for shells, with about nine miles of Gulf beach in the description.
  • Grayton Beach State Park. Grayton Beach is the Walton County state park with a mile of beach, beach mats, a wheelchair you arrange ahead, and no lifeguards.
  • Topsail Hill Preserve State Park. Topsail Hill is the Walton preserve with 3.2 miles of beach, a tram, and beach wheelchairs. The page talks about sea beans, not shells.
  • Deer Lake State Park. Deer Lake is the smaller Walton state park: a half mile of beach, a boardwalk, shells mentioned, and no lifeguards.
  • Perdido Key State Park. Perdido Key State Park is the Escambia Gulf beach the page ties to winter shelling, with chairs requested through Big Lagoon.
  • St. Andrews State Park. St. Andrews is the Panama City state park with more than a mile and a half of beach, a jetty, and a ferry onward to Shell Island.
  • Shell Island. Shell Island is the barrier island St. Andrews sends you to by ferry or private boat. The page does not inventory the island's restrooms or chairs.
  • Camp Helen State Park. Camp Helen is the Bay County state park between the Gulf and Lake Powell, with a beach wheelchair and no lifeguards.
  • St. George Island State Park. St. George Island State Park is the Franklin County Gulf park with 9 miles of beach in the description, mats at named accesses, and several kinds of beach chair.
  • Bald Point Sunrise Beach. Sunrise Beach is one of two named Bald Point beaches. The page gives it a mat, showers, and a shared beach wheelchair.
  • Bald Point North End Beach. North End Beach is the other Bald Point swim, with a mat and a fishing pier the page also fits with a mat.

Questions people ask

Which beach has the best sunset?

We do not rank sunsets. Gulf beaches have the sun setting over the open water when the sky is clear. Read the park page for access and parking.

Do Atlantic beaches get a sunset over the water?

No. They face east. The morning light is the ocean light.
