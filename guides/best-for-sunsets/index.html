Where the sun sets over the water

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 20 Nov 2026

Direction, not a ranking. On the Gulf the open water is to the west, so a clear evening can put the sun over the water. Atlantic beaches in this guide face the other way. We do not score the view. Weather, a dune, or a building can still block it.

Only three park pages we use actually mention sunset. The other 52 Gulf beaches face the same water and stay silent about it. They are listed on the Gulf Coast region, not repeated here. Copying that whole list onto this page made the two pages read like the same article.

Coquina Beach

Coquina Beach is the Manatee County page whose introduction talks about the sun setting over the water. That sentence was recorded on our Coquina page on 16 Nov 2026 and was not reopened today. The county lifeguard page names Coquina as one of three guarded beaches. The location page says pets are prohibited and lists beach wheelchairs. It does not state a parking price or a space count.

Delnor-Wiggins Pass

Delnor-Wiggins Pass was rechecked on 20 Nov 2026. The picnicking section calls it a lunch or sunset picnic spot. The swimming section is a different fact: dangerous currents and riptides can occur in Wiggins Pass, so swimming runs from the southern boundary to the double red flags at the northern end of parking area four. Those flags are a boundary the park prints, not a forecast we update. The page also tells visitors to read warning flag signs at each parking area entrance. It says lifeguards are not provided. We do not repeat the park's green flag wording.

Henderson Beach

Henderson Beach was rechecked the same day. The beach section says a visit can include a sunset. The swimming section says to read the warning flag signs at the welcome stations and on the beach, and it does not say a lifeguard is on duty. The parking section still lists 422 day use spaces, plus 24 recreational vehicle spaces and 20 accessible spaces. Pets are not allowed on the sand.

Questions people ask

Which beach has the best sunset?

We do not rank sunsets. Three park pages mention sunset: Coquina, Delnor-Wiggins, and Henderson. Other Gulf beaches face west and do not say so. Read the park page for access and parking.

Do Atlantic beaches get a sunset over the water?

No. They face east. Anastasia is an Atlantic page in this guide. The morning light is the ocean light.
