Which Florida beaches allow dogs on the sand?

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 19 Nov 2026

How this list is made. The sand allows a dog only when the managing agency says so. A dog park next to a beach is not the beach. Service animals are not pets, and the park pages describe them separately.

We rechecked the linked county and Florida State Parks pages on 19 Nov 2026. The five beaches below still match a published dog rule for sand or a named pet beach. Most Florida State Parks pages we checked still keep pets off the beach. Honeymoon Island remains the clear state park exception, with a pet beach at the southern tip. County rules stay local. Read the note on each beach page before you unload the dog.

Beaches with a published dog rule

  • Maxine Barritt Park. Maxine Barritt Park is one of two Sarasota County beach listings marked dog friendly with a leash required. It also lists swimming, fishing, restrooms and a paved trail.
  • Siesta Key Beach Access 1. Siesta Key Beach Access 1 is the island access the county marks dog friendly with a leash required. The directory also lists swimming, birding and wildlife viewing, and it does not list a lifeguard or a restroom.
  • Fort De Soto Park. Fort De Soto is the Pinellas County park with miles of Gulf beach, two swim centers, and the only county dog beach.
  • Honeymoon Island State Park. Honeymoon Island is the Pinellas state park with a named pet beach, shelling on the north spit, and free beach wheelchairs.
  • Anclote Key Preserve State Park. Anclote Key is a boat or ferry preserve in Pasco, with no car lot and no lifeguard on duty. Dogs are limited to North Anclote Bar.

Sarasota County's beach directory still marks Maxine Barritt Park and Siesta Key Beach Access 1 as dog friendly with a leash required. The same directory lists other Siesta accesses without that mark, and the Siesta Beach destination page still allows only service animals on that main beach.

Pinellas County still says dogs are not allowed on county beaches except the dog beach at Fort De Soto, reached from the dog park. The county page also notes that Fort De Soto ranked among top dog friendly beaches in a 2024 list; that ranking is not our score, and the sand rule is still the designated dog beach only.

Anclote Key Preserve still bars pets on Anclote Key itself. Pets are permitted only on North Anclote Bar, on a six foot handheld leash, per the state park page.

Questions people ask

Can I bring my dog to any Florida beach?

No. Many county and state park pages prohibit pets on the sand. Use this list, then read the beach page.

Are service animals allowed?

Park pages we cite say service animals are welcome more broadly than pets. Confirm the definition on that agency's page.

Is a dog park the same as a dog beach?

No. A fenced dog park next to the sand is not permission to walk the open beach. Fort De Soto is the Pinellas exception with a designated dog beach reached from its dog park.
