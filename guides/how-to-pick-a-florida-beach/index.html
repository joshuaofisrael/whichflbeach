How to pick a Florida beach

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 21 Nov 2026

Short version. Choose the constraint that would ruin the day, then open that beach's page and the agency link. We do not rank a single winner.

1. Parking and arrival

Read whether the lot is free, metered, or covered by a park entrance fee, and whether the agency says the lot fills. If the page does not give a clock time, we do not invent one. Have a second beach in the same county. The free parking guide names the pages that say free, the pages that print a fee, and the beaches with no car lot.

2. Dogs, kids, and step free access

Dog rules change at the county line. A beach wheelchair or an access mat is listed only when the park page says so. Lifeguard coverage is listed only when the page says who is on duty, and for which hours.

3. Shells

Empty shells and live animals are different things. Read the shelling guide and the FWC page before you pocket anything.

4. The day you go

Red tide and bacteria are not facts we freeze on a page. Use the red tide guide and the health department map that morning. Beach warning flags are a different sign. The flag color guide explains the state system and does not post today's color.

Questions people ask

What is the best Florida beach?

There is no single best beach. The useful question is which published constraint matters: a guarded swim, a dog, a beach wheelchair, shells, or a lot that the agency says fills.

Do you post today's red tide?

No. FWC posts the current status and a daily sample map. We link those pages and explain how to read them.
