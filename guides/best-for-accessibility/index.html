Beaches with a wheelchair or an access mat

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

How this list is made. A beach is here when the managing agency page says it has a beach wheelchair, a swimming chair, or a mat onto the sand. We do not infer access from a paved lot alone.

Chairs are often first come, first served, and some parks ask you to reserve. The page says where to ask. We do not publish a phone number here. Use the link on the beach page. Anastasia names a Mobi-Mat and elevated boardwalks, with free chairs at the beach shop or Anastasia Water Sports. Siesta Beach is the page that states a mat length: 504 feet toward the Gulf.

Beaches

  • Siesta Beach. Siesta Beach is the Sarasota County park with the large free lot, a published lifeguard window, a free beach wheelchair and a 504 foot access mat.
  • Coquina Beach. Coquina Beach is a Manatee County beach on Anna Maria with a lifeguard station, beach wheelchairs, a playground, and an introduction that talks about the sun setting over the water.
  • Manatee Public Beach. Manatee Public Beach is the Holmes Beach county park with a published all year lifeguard schedule and beach wheelchairs on the amenity list.
  • Honeymoon Island State Park. Honeymoon Island is the Pinellas state park with a named pet beach, shelling on the north spit, and free beach wheelchairs.
  • Caladesi Island State Park. Caladesi is the island park reached by ferry from Honeymoon Island. The page promotes shelling and bans live shells and pets on the beach.
  • Stump Pass Beach State Park. Stump Pass is the Charlotte County state park beach the page ties to winter shelling, with free wheelchairs and no lifeguards.
  • Don Pedro Island State Park. Don Pedro is a boat only Gulf beach in Charlotte County. The drive up land base is not the swim.
  • Lovers Key State Park. Lovers Key is the Lee County state park that calls its own beach some of the best shelling on that coast, and that warns the lot fills on busy weekends.
  • Gasparilla Island State Park. Gasparilla Island State Park is the Boca Grande Gulf beach where the page highlights winter shelling and several named lots.
  • Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park. Delnor Wiggins is the Collier state park the page credits with some of the best Gulf shelling, four beach lots, and both manual and electric beach wheelchairs.
  • Grayton Beach State Park. Grayton Beach is the Walton County state park with a mile of beach, beach mats, a wheelchair you arrange ahead, and no lifeguards.
  • Topsail Hill Preserve State Park. Topsail Hill is the Walton preserve with 3.2 miles of beach, a tram, and beach wheelchairs. The page talks about sea beans, not shells.
  • Henderson Beach State Park. Henderson Beach is the Destin state park with 422 day use spaces, a beach wheelchair, and a page that mentions sunset.
  • Perdido Key State Park. Perdido Key State Park is the Escambia Gulf beach the page ties to winter shelling, with chairs requested through Big Lagoon.
  • Big Lagoon State Park. Big Lagoon is the Pensacola state park the page is careful to call an Intracoastal beach, not a Gulf beach.
  • St. Andrews State Park. St. Andrews is the Panama City state park with more than a mile and a half of beach, a jetty, and a ferry onward to Shell Island.
  • Camp Helen State Park. Camp Helen is the Bay County state park between the Gulf and Lake Powell, with a beach wheelchair and no lifeguards.
  • St. George Island State Park. St. George Island State Park is the Franklin County Gulf park with 9 miles of beach in the description, mats at named accesses, and several kinds of beach chair.
  • Bald Point Sunrise Beach. Sunrise Beach is one of two named Bald Point beaches. The page gives it a mat, showers, and a shared beach wheelchair.
  • Bald Point North End Beach. North End Beach is the other Bald Point swim, with a mat and a fishing pier the page also fits with a mat.
  • Anastasia State Park. Anastasia State Park has four miles of beach, a fee that covers 350 spaces, a Mobi-Mat, and lifeguards only from Memorial Day through Labor Day.
  • Little Talbot Island State Park. Little Talbot is the Duval barrier island park with shelling, fossil shark teeth on the park's own list, two beach wheelchairs, and no lifeguards.
  • Sebastian Inlet State Park. Sebastian Inlet is the Atlantic park with a protected cove the page recommends for children, plus surf, beachcombing, and no lifeguards.
  • Fort Pierce Inlet State Park. Fort Pierce Inlet State Park is the St. Lucie beach with a firm bottom, shells, snorkeling, and a free wheelchair you request at the gate.
  • John D. MacArthur Beach State Park. MacArthur Beach is the Palm Beach County state park with nearly two miles of Atlantic beach, three free wheelchairs, and shelling in the park's own list.
  • Hugh Taylor Birch State Park. Hugh Taylor Birch is the Fort Lauderdale state park that opens onto the city beach, with 252 spaces and a beach wheelchair at the ranger station.
  • Crandon Park Beach. Crandon Park is the Key Biscayne county beach with a published parking price, a lot under 3,000 spaces, and beach wheelchairs.
  • Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park. Bill Baggs is the Cape Florida park on Key Biscayne with swimming chairs, beach chairs, and a mat at access 6.
  • Loggerhead Beach at Bahia Honda. Loggerhead Beach is the Bahia Honda shore the park page says faces the Atlantic, with shallow water.
  • Calusa Beach at Bahia Honda. Calusa Beach is the Bahia Honda shore the park page says faces Florida Bay, with two covered picnic pavilions.
  • Sandspur Beach at Bahia Honda. Sandspur Beach is the Bahia Honda shore with parking along the beach, facing the Atlantic, and a centralized restroom.
  • Fort Zachary Taylor Historic State Park. Fort Zachary Taylor is the Key West state park the page calls the best swimming on the island, with snorkeling from shore and a rocky bottom.
  • Cannon Beach at John Pennekamp. Cannon Beach is the Pennekamp shore the page calls the main snorkel beach, with shipwreck artifacts placed in shallow water.
  • Far Beach at John Pennekamp. Far Beach is the other Pennekamp beach, which the page describes as a place to swim or sit under the palms rather than as the artifact snorkel spot.
  • Long Key State Park. Long Key State Park is the Atlantic shore with a beach wheelchair, shallow swimming, and a birding trail. The page does not describe shelling.

Check another beach

A paved lot is not a mat. Siesta states 504 feet. Anastasia names a Mobi-Mat and does not state a length. Honeymoon Island offers free chairs and a pet beach that is not the main swim.

Questions people ask

Is the chair free?

When the park page says there is no charge, the beach page repeats that. If the page is silent, we do not guess.

Does a mat reach the water?

Only if the park page describes the length or the route. Siesta Beach is one page that states a length.
