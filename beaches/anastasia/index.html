Anastasia State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 20 Nov 2026

St. Augustine · St. Johns County · Florida State Parks

LifeguardsAccessShellsKids

Quick read. It suits a summer swim in the designated area behind the beach shop, a low tide shell walk, and a visitor who needs the mat or a free chair. Outside that window the page does not promise a lifeguard. Overnight parking is not allowed.

Anastasia State Park has four miles of beach, a fee that covers 350 spaces, a Mobi-Mat, and lifeguards only from Memorial Day through Labor Day.

Rechecked 20 Nov 2026 against the experiences page and the hours and fees page. The new planning facts are the dollar amounts, the 8 a.m. to sundown day, and the ban on overnight parking. The 350 spaces and the weekend fill note were already on the experiences page. The page still does not name a clock time for when the lot fills.

The beach is open to bicycles and people on foot. Motorized vehicles are not allowed, and visitors may not enter the dunes. Shelling is tied to low tide. The park says it hosts some of the better surfing on the east coast of Florida and tells swimmers to heed posted warnings, including rip currents. We do not post today's flag or this week's water. Use the red tide guide for where current samples live.

North Peninsula is the next state park north in this guide, with shelling and a page that says there is no lifeguard on duty. Little Talbot Island lists two beach wheelchairs and no lifeguards. Anastasia is the one with the summer guard window and a stated day use fee.

What the managing agency publishes

Managed byFlorida State Parks
PlaceSt. Augustine, St. Johns County
Open waterAtlantic
ParkingThe hours page says the park is open 8 a.m. until sundown, 365 days a year. Admission is 8 dollars per vehicle for up to eight people, 4 dollars for a single occupant vehicle, and 2 dollars for a pedestrian, a bicyclist, or an extra passenger. The experiences page says there are 350 spaces, parking is included with admission, overnight parking is not permitted, and the lot tends to fill on busy weekends and holidays. It suggests returning an hour or two later. It does not give a clock time.
When the lot fillsBusy weekends and holidays, with no clock time. The experiences page suggests trying again an hour or two later. Overnight parking is not permitted.
ShellingThe page says beachcombing is a favorite and names clam, scallop, oyster and olive shells. It says to plan around low tide, and that most of what you find was or could still be alive.
DogsThe experiences page says pets are prohibited from the beach. The accessible amenities section says service animals are welcome in all areas of Florida State Parks.
LifeguardsA designated swimming area at the beach access ramp behind Island Beach Shop and Grill is monitored by a lifeguard from Memorial Day through Labor Day. The page says swimmers should exercise caution whether or not a lifeguard is present.
RestroomsThe day use area has three restrooms. Beach and Salt Run restrooms have outdoor showers. One playground is in the beach access area. Two more accessible playgrounds are in the campground loops.
Beach wheelchairBeach wheelchairs are free. Ask at the Island Beach Shop and Grill or at Anastasia Water Sports.
Access matThe accessible list names a Mobi-Mat to the beach, plus elevated boardwalks. It does not state the mat length.

Fees and hours change. If a dollar amount or a clock time is not in the table, the page we checked did not state it. Confirm on the linked agency page before you go.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Anastasia State Park?

The experiences page says pets are prohibited from the beach. It also says service animals are welcome in all areas of Florida State Parks.

Are there lifeguards at Anastasia State Park?

A designated swim at the ramp behind Island Beach Shop and Grill is monitored from Memorial Day through Labor Day. The page says to exercise caution whether or not a lifeguard is present. Outside that window the page does not promise a lifeguard.

What does parking cost at Anastasia?

The hours page checked 20 Nov 2026 lists 8 dollars per vehicle for up to eight people, 4 dollars for a single occupant vehicle, and 2 dollars for a pedestrian, a bicyclist, or an extra passenger. The experiences page says that fee covers 350 spaces and that overnight parking is not permitted.

Is there a beach wheelchair or a mat?

Beach wheelchairs are free at the Island Beach Shop and Grill or Anastasia Water Sports. The accessible list names a Mobi-Mat and elevated boardwalks. It does not state the mat length.
