Anastasia State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

St. Augustine · St. Johns County · Florida State Parks

Anastasia is the St. Augustine state park with four miles of beach, a seasonal lifeguard window, a mat, and shelling the page describes.

What the managing agency publishes

Managed byFlorida State Parks
PlaceSt. Augustine, St. Johns County
Open waterAtlantic
ParkingThe page says there are 350 spaces, parking is included with admission, and the lot tends to fill on busy weekends and holidays. It suggests returning an hour or two later. It does not give a clock time.
When the lot fillsBusy weekends and holidays, with no clock time. The page suggests trying again later.
ShellingThe page says beachcombing is a favorite and names clam, scallop, oyster and olive shells. It says most of what you find was or could still be alive.
DogsThe page says pets are prohibited from the beach.
LifeguardsA designated swimming area behind the beach shop is monitored by a lifeguard from Memorial Day through Labor Day. The page says to be careful whether or not a lifeguard is present.
RestroomsThe day use area has three restrooms. Beach and Salt Run restrooms have outdoor showers. A playground is in the beach access area.
Beach wheelchairBeach wheelchairs are free. Ask at the Island Beach Shop and Grill or at Anastasia Watersports.
Access matThe page says a mat allows access to the beach, along with elevated boardwalks.

Fees and hours change. If a dollar amount or a clock time is not in the table, the page we checked did not state it. Confirm on the linked agency page before you go.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.
