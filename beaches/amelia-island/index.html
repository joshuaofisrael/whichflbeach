Amelia Island State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 18 Nov 2026

Fernandina Beach · Nassau County · Florida State Parks

Shells

Quick read. It suits an undeveloped, horseback-friendly Atlantic beach for shelling and surf fishing. It does not suit a swimmer who needs a lifeguard, which the page says are not provided.

Amelia Island State Park is the Nassau County state park where the page says visitors may bring their own horse onto the beach, calling it one of the few Florida beaches where that is allowed.

A rider bringing a personal horse must contact the ranger station ahead, and daily capacity limits may apply, the page says. It also marks seasonal shorebird-nesting closures, especially at the southern point, from March through September.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.
