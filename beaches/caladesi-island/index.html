Caladesi Island State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Dunedin · Pinellas County · Florida State Parks

AccessShellsGulf sunset direction

Quick read. It suits a shell walk and a swim without a dog. It does not suit someone who needs a published lifeguard promise.

Caladesi is the island park reached by ferry from Honeymoon Island. The page promotes shelling and bans live shells and pets on the beach.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Caladesi Island State Park?

The page says pets are not allowed on the beach at any time, and they are not allowed on the ferry.

Are there lifeguards at Caladesi Island State Park?

The swimming section does not say whether lifeguards are on duty. Do not assume the Honeymoon Island rule applies here. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Caladesi Island State Park?

The park page says a beach wheelchair is available at no charge. Ask at the ranger station. We do not print a phone number. The page lists elevated boardwalks, not a mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Caladesi Island State Park?

Most visitors arrive by the ferry from Honeymoon Island or by boat. The page lists marina dock fees. The experiences page does not list the day use entrance fee. Check the park fees page before you go. The page does not describe a car lot on the island.
