Caspersen Beach

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 20 Nov 2026

Venice · Sarasota County · Sarasota County Parks

Gulf sunset direction

Quick read. It suits a walk or a bike ride in from South Brohard when you do not need a restroom, a playground, or a lifeguard on the Caspersen list. It does not suit a car.

Caspersen Beach is open on foot and by bicycle. The county says the vehicle lot, restrooms, and playground have been closed since the 2024 storm washout.

The practical change is the entrance, not a new amenity list. On 20 Nov 2026 the county facility page still listed beach access, bicycling, birding, a canoe or kayak launch, fishing, grills, outdoor fitness equipment, picnicking, swimming, an unpaved trail, and wildlife viewing. In the same paragraph it said the Harbor Drive access washed out during the 2024 storm season and that restrooms, vehicle parking, and the playground stay closed until repairs. Entry is from South Brohard Park or the Venetian Waterway Park Trail at the City of Venice Festival Grounds. We do not print the street address or a phone number. Use the county link.

South Brohard is the car access the county names. Its directory line still lists a restroom. Caspersen's own restroom is the one the facility page says is closed. Venice Beach is the nearby listing that includes a lifeguard. Service Club Park is the picnic and volleyball listing, also without a lifeguard in the directory. None of those pages is a promise about the water.

People ask Caspersen about fossil teeth. The facility page we checked does not mention teeth or shells, so this page does not either. If a later county page adds that, the shelling guide is where it would belong. The Sarasota County hub is the rest of the directory.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I park a car at Caspersen Beach?

Not on the facility page checked 20 Nov 2026. Vehicle parking is closed. The county says to enter on foot or by bicycle from South Brohard Park or the Venetian Waterway Park Trail.

Are the Caspersen restrooms and playground open?

The same page says restrooms, vehicle parking, and the playground are closed until repairs. Hours printed there are 6 a.m. to 10 p.m.

Is there a lifeguard at Caspersen Beach?

Lifeguard is not in the amenity list. Treat the water as unguarded unless a sign on site says otherwise.

Does the county page mention shark teeth at Caspersen?

No. The facility page does not mention fossil teeth or shelling. This guide does not add that from a tourism site.
