Far Beach at John Pennekamp

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Key Largo · Monroe County · Florida State Parks

Access

Quick read. It suits a quieter swim in the same park. Cannon Beach is the one with the placed shipwreck pieces.

Far Beach is the other Pennekamp beach, which the page describes as a place to swim or sit under the palms rather than as the artifact snorkel spot.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

The experiences page says pets are permitted under the pet policy and does not spell out the beach in the section we used. Many state park pages ban pets on beaches. Confirm at the ranger station before you bring a dog.

The page says there are no lifeguards and swimming is at your own risk. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

A beach wheelchair is available on request at the visitor center. The page we checked does not list an access mat.
