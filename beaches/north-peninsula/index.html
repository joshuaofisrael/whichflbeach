North Peninsula State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Flagler Beach · St. Johns County · Florida State Parks

Shells

Quick read. It suits a surf or shell walk you supervise yourself. It does not list a beach wheelchair.

North Peninsula is the Atlantic park north of the Flagler line in this guide, with 2.5 miles of beach, shelling, surfing, and no lifeguard.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to North Peninsula State Park?

The page says pets are not allowed on the beach or in buildings.

Are there lifeguards at North Peninsula State Park?

The surfing section says there is no lifeguard on duty and that staff patrol the beach. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at North Peninsula State Park?

The page we checked does not list a beach wheelchair. The page lists an accessible boardwalk and ramp, not a mat on the sand.

What does the agency say about parking at North Peninsula State Park?

The experiences page does not list the day use entrance fee. Check the park fees page before you go. The page does not give a space count. The page does not say what time the lot fills.
