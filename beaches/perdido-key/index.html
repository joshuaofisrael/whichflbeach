Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Perdido Key State Park?

The Perdido Key page we checked does not print a pet rule. Big Lagoon, the sister park, says Escambia County prohibits pets on beaches. Confirm Perdido before you bring a dog.

Are there lifeguards at Perdido Key State Park?

The park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Perdido Key State Park?

The page says beach wheelchairs are free and that you request one through the ranger station at Big Lagoon State Park. The page lists boardwalks, not a mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Perdido Key State Park?

The page says two lots along the highway are free with paid admission. The experiences page does not list the day use entrance fee. Check the park fees page before you go. The page does not say what time the lot fills.
