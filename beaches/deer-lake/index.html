Deer Lake State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Santa Rosa Beach · Walton County · Florida State Parks

Quick read. It suits a short shell walk and a swim you supervise. It does not list a beach wheelchair.

Deer Lake is the smaller Walton state park: a half mile of beach, a boardwalk, shells mentioned, and no lifeguards.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Deer Lake State Park?

The page says pets are not allowed on the boardwalk, the beaches, or in buildings.

Are there lifeguards at Deer Lake State Park?

The park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Deer Lake State Park?

The page we checked does not list a beach wheelchair. The page lists a boardwalk to the beach, not a mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Deer Lake State Park?

The page says a paved lot leads to the boardwalk, picnic shelter and restroom. The experiences page does not list the day use entrance fee. Check the park fees page before you go. The page does not say what time the lot fills.
