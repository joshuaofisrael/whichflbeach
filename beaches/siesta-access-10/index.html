Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Siesta Key Beach Access 10?

The county directory does not mark this beach dog friendly. The Siesta Beach page allows only service animals on that beach. Confirm this beach on the county dog page before you bring a pet.

Are there lifeguards at Siesta Key Beach Access 10?

Lifeguard is not in the amenity list the county publishes for this beach. Treat the water as unguarded unless a sign on site says otherwise. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Siesta Key Beach Access 10?

The page we checked does not list a beach wheelchair. The page we checked does not list an access mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Siesta Key Beach Access 10?

The county hours page says this access does not provide parking and is open to pedestrians at all hours. Dry sand at county beaches is otherwise listed as 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. There is no lot on the directory title.
