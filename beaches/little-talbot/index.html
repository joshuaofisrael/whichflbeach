Little Talbot Island State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Jacksonville · Duval County · Florida State Parks

AccessShells

Quick read. It suits a long Atlantic walk and a shell hunt. The page says not to swim at the Fort George River inlet at the south end.

Little Talbot is the Duval barrier island park with shelling, fossil shark teeth on the park's own list, two beach wheelchairs, and no lifeguards.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Little Talbot Island State Park?

The page says pets are not allowed on the beaches or the boardwalks.

Are there lifeguards at Little Talbot Island State Park?

The park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

The accessible list includes two beach wheelchairs. The page lists boardwalks, not a mat.
