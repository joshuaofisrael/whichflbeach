Henderson Beach State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 21 Nov 2026

Destin · Okaloosa County · Florida State Parks

AccessKidsGulf sunset direction

Quick read. It suits a large lot and a Gulf evening. The page does not promise a lifeguard. Dogs stay off the sand.

Henderson Beach is the Destin state park with 422 day use spaces, a beach wheelchair, and a page that mentions sunset.

The day use price is on the hours page, not the experiences page. The figures in the parking line were rechecked 21 Nov 2026. The free parking guide puts this 6 dollar admission next to the lots that charge nothing.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Henderson Beach State Park?

The page says no animals are allowed on the beach. Pets may be in the lots, on the nature trail, and on the day use sidewalk, on a leash no longer than six feet.

Are there lifeguards at Henderson Beach State Park?

The swimming section describes flags and does not say whether lifeguards are on duty. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Henderson Beach State Park?

The page says an all terrain beach wheelchair is available. Ask at the ranger station. The page we checked does not list an access mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Henderson Beach State Park?

The experiences page says the day use area has 422 spaces, plus 24 recreational vehicle spaces and 20 accessible spaces, and it does not print the day use fee. The hours page checked 21 Nov 2026 lists 6 dollars per vehicle for two to eight people, 4 dollars for a single occupant vehicle, and 2 dollars for a pedestrian, a bicyclist, or an extra passenger.
