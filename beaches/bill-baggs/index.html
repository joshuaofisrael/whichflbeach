Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Key Biscayne · Miami-Dade County · Florida State Parks

Access

Quick read. It suits an Atlantic swim at the south end of Key Biscayne if you do not need a lifeguard. Crandon is the county beach with the printed parking price.

Bill Baggs is the Cape Florida park on Key Biscayne with swimming chairs, beach chairs, and a mat at access 6.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park?

The page says pets are not allowed on the beach, in wetlands, on the playground, or in the lighthouse and the cafes.

Are there lifeguards at Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park?

The page says there are no lifeguards and swimming is at your own risk. It also says to watch for Portuguese man of war warnings in winter. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park?

Two beach wheelchairs and two swimming wheelchairs are free and first come, first served, at the bicycle rental. A beach mat is at beach access 6, next to the cafe.
