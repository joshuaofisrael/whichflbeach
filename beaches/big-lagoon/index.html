Big Lagoon State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 21 Nov 2026

Pensacola · Escambia County · Florida State Parks

AccessKids

Quick read. It suits a calm bay swim, a playground, and a beach wheelchair. It is the wrong page if you want a Gulf sunset or a dog on the sand.

Big Lagoon is the Pensacola state park the page is careful to call an Intracoastal beach, not a Gulf beach.

The experiences page still does not print the day use fee. The hours page does, and those figures were rechecked 21 Nov 2026. The free parking guide lines this 6 dollar admission up against Siesta's free lot and Big Talbot's 3 dollar lots.

What the managing agency publishes

Managed byFlorida State Parks
PlacePensacola, Escambia County
Open waterBay or Intracoastal
ParkingThe experiences page says parking is at the west beach and the amphitheater and does not print a price. The hours page checked 21 Nov 2026 lists 6 dollars per vehicle for two to eight people, 4 dollars for a single occupant vehicle, and 2 dollars for a pedestrian, a bicyclist, or an extra passenger. The same hours page lists a boat launch at 10 dollars for one person with a boat and 12 dollars for two to eight people with a boat.
When the lot fillsThe page does not say what time the lot fills.
ShellingThe page we checked does not describe shelling.
DogsThe page says Escambia County prohibits pets on beaches and waterways. Pets are allowed in designated areas, including the campground.
LifeguardsThe park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign.
RestroomsThe page says restrooms are at the west beach and the east beach, with outdoor showers at picnic areas.
Beach wheelchairThe page says beach wheelchairs are free at the ranger station.
Access matThe page we checked does not list an access mat.

Fees and hours change. If a dollar amount or a clock time is not in the table, the page we checked did not state it. Confirm on the linked agency page before you go.

Red tide and water quality

This is a bay or Intracoastal shore. FWC says Florida red tide can enter bays and estuaries, and that Karenia brevis does not persist in freshwater. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Big Lagoon State Park?

The page says Escambia County prohibits pets on beaches and waterways. Pets are allowed in designated areas, including the campground.

Are there lifeguards at Big Lagoon State Park?

The park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Big Lagoon State Park?

The page says beach wheelchairs are free at the ranger station. The page we checked does not list an access mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Big Lagoon State Park?

The experiences page says parking is at the west beach and the amphitheater and does not print a price. The hours page checked 21 Nov 2026 lists 6 dollars per vehicle for two to eight people, 4 dollars for a single occupant vehicle, and 2 dollars for a pedestrian, a bicyclist, or an extra passenger. The same hours page lists a boat launch at 10 dollars for one person with a boat and 12 dollars for two to eight people with a boat. The page does not say what time the lot fills.
