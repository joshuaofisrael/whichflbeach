Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Bald Point North End Beach?

The Bald Point page says pets are not allowed on the beach.

Are there lifeguards at Bald Point North End Beach?

The page says lifeguards are not provided. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Bald Point North End Beach?

The park wheelchair is shared. The page says you can reserve it. We do not print the phone number. The page says North End Beach has a beach mat. A fishing pier at this entrance also has a mat.
