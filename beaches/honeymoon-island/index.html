Honeymoon Island State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Quick read. It suits a dog only on the pet beach, a shell walk on the north end, and a swim where you accept that lifeguards are not provided.

Honeymoon Island is the Pinellas state park with a named pet beach, shelling on the north spit, and free beach wheelchairs.

The page says the main beach is the swimming area and that the shoreline is about four miles.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Honeymoon Island State Park?

The park page says dogs are allowed on the pet beach at the southern tip and on the nature trail, on a six foot handheld leash. That is not the main swimming beach.

Are there lifeguards at Honeymoon Island State Park?

The park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Honeymoon Island State Park?

The page says beach wheelchairs are free and that you ask at the ranger station. The page lists elevated boardwalks to the beach. It does not call them an access mat.
