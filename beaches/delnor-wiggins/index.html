Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 20 Nov 2026

Naples · Collier County · Florida State Parks

AccessShellsGulf sunset direction

Quick read. It suits a shell day and a visitor who needs a chair if they arrive when one is free. It does not suit someone who needs a flush restroom or a lifeguard.

Delnor Wiggins is the Collier state park the page credits with some of the best Gulf shelling, four beach lots, and both manual and electric beach wheelchairs.

Rechecked 20 Nov 2026. The swimming section says dangerous currents and riptides can occur in Wiggins Pass, so swimming runs from the southern boundary to the double red flags at the northern end of parking area four. The fishing section says that northern stretch is closed to water entry and anglers must stay on dry land. Warning flag signs are posted at each parking area entrance and on the beach. The park's flag list includes a green clearance this site will not repeat. Read the sign at the lot. We do not post today's color. The picnicking section still calls the park a lunch or sunset picnic spot, and the page says lifeguards are not provided.

That double red line is a boundary in the park text, not a daily forecast. The sunset guide is where this page sits next to Coquina, which has a county lifeguard listing, and Henderson Beach, which mentions sunset and does not say a lifeguard is on duty.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park?

The page says pets are not permitted on the beach. A six foot leash applies on paved areas.

Are there lifeguards at Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park?

The park page says lifeguards are not provided. Swim at your own risk and read the flag sign. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park?

Manual beach wheelchairs are in parking areas one and three, first come, first served, at no charge. An electric beach wheelchair is also free.
