Siesta Beach

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 19 Nov 2026

Siesta Key · Sarasota County · Sarasota County Parks

LifeguardsAccessKidsGulf sunset direction

Quick read. It suits a group that wants a staffed Gulf swim, a playground, food, and step free help, and that can use the free trolley if the lot is busy. It does not suit someone bringing a pet dog.

Siesta Beach is the Sarasota County park with the large free lot, a published lifeguard window, a free beach wheelchair and a 504 foot access mat.

The county also lists a shaded playground, concessions, sand volleyball, pickleball and tennis. Alcohol is allowed for people of legal drinking age. Glass is prohibited. The page links out for red tide instead of describing current conditions.

ParkingThe Siesta Beach page says there are over 950 free spaces, including recreational vehicles, motorcycles and accessible spaces. The county hours page says that lot is open year round from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. The same page says the Siesta Key Breeze trolley is free and runs 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day, with modified hours on some holidays.
When the lot fillsThe county pages we checked do not say what time the lot fills. They do call this the most parking of any public beach in the county, and they call the beach a top tourist stop.
ShellingThe Siesta Beach page describes nearly 100 percent quartz crystal sand and says it does not get hot. It does not describe shelling.
DogsThe Siesta Beach page says only service animals are allowed on Siesta Beach.
LifeguardsThe Sarasota County beaches page says lifeguards are on duty 10 a.m. to 4:45 p.m., seven days a week, at six named beaches including Siesta Beach.
RestroomsThe Siesta Beach page says there are plenty of restrooms and no dedicated changing rooms.
Beach wheelchairThe Siesta Beach page says beach wheelchairs are free, year round, from the parking areas to the shore, and that they are signed out at the main pavilion concession. Users complete a liability form. The page tells you to ask about availability. We do not print the phone number here.
Access matThe Sarasota County beaches page says an access mat at Siesta Beach extends 504 feet toward the Gulf.

Fees and hours change. If a dollar amount or a clock time is not in the table, the page we checked did not state it. Confirm on the linked agency page before you go.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Siesta Beach?

The Siesta Beach page says only service animals are allowed on Siesta Beach.

Are there lifeguards at Siesta Beach?

The Sarasota County beaches page says lifeguards are on duty 10 a.m. to 4:45 p.m., seven days a week, at six named beaches including Siesta Beach. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Siesta Beach?

The Siesta Beach page says beach wheelchairs are free, year round, from the parking areas to the shore, and that they are signed out at the main pavilion concession. Users complete a liability form.
