Big Talbot Island State Park

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 18 Nov 2026

Jacksonville · Duval County · Florida State Parks

Shells

Quick read. It suits a driftwood and photography walk, not a swim: the park page itself says swimming is not recommended here because of strong currents and marine wildlife. Little Talbot Island, just south in the same park group, is the swim beach.

Big Talbot Island is the Duval County state park behind Boneyard Beach, where the page describes salt-bleached, fallen live oak and cedar trees and black, rock-like outcroppings along three miles of shoreline. Collecting the driftwood is prohibited.

Two trails reach the beach: the Shoreline Trail from the Bluffs picnic area, and the Black Rock Trail off A1A, which the page says leads to the black, rock-like outcroppings.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Atlantic. FWC says some Gulf blooms have been carried by the Gulf Stream into the Atlantic, as far as Delaware, so an east coast beach is not automatically clear. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.
