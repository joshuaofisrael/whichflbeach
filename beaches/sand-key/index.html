Sand Key is the Clearwater county beach with paid parking, a dog park that is not the beach, and seasonal lifeguards named in a county notice.

Red tide and water quality

This beach is on the Gulf, the coast where FWC says Florida red tides were documented in the 1840s and where blooms most often form in late summer and early fall. We do not post this week's conditions. FWC updates a daily sample map and a status report, and it points readers to the Mote beach conditions reports. The Florida Department of Health posts bacteria results and advisories on its Healthy Beaches map. Read those before you swim, and read the longer background on our red tide guide.

Questions people ask

Can I bring a dog to Sand Key Park?

Sand Key has a dog park. The county FAQ says dogs are not allowed on county beaches except the Fort De Soto dog beach. The dog park is not permission to use the sand.

Are there lifeguards at Sand Key Park?

The same seasonal lifeguard notice that covers Fort De Soto names Sand Key from April to September. The parks FAQ says to check signs because coverage is seasonal. A lifeguard schedule can change. Read the agency page the week you go.

Is there a beach wheelchair or an access mat at Sand Key Park?

The page we checked does not list a beach wheelchair. The page we checked does not list an access mat.

What does the agency say about parking at Sand Key Park?

The park page says hourly and daily parking rates apply and that annual beach passes exist. It does not print the dollar amount. The page says the park is open 7 a.m. to sunset. The page does not say what time the lot fills.
