Sources and methods

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Managing agencies

Beach facts come from the county or city parks page, or from the Florida State Parks experiences and amenities page for that park. The link is on the beach page. We checked those pages on 16 Nov 2026.

What we copy into the table

Parking, including a price only when the page states one. Whether the agency says the lot fills. Shelling only when the page mentions shells or beachcombing. Dog rules in the words the page supports. Lifeguards only when the page says they are on duty or that they are not provided. Restrooms, beach wheelchairs and access mats the same way. If the page is silent, the table says so.

What we never freeze

Current red tide cell counts, current bacteria advisories, and temporary closures. FWC and the Florida Department of Health publish those. Our red tide guide explains the history and the links.

Shells and health

Shell rules follow the FWC recreational sea shell page. Health warnings about red tide are attributed to FWC or the health department. They are not medical advice from this site.

Our read of who a beach suits

The "who it suits" line is our planning note from the published facts. It is labeled that way. It is not a rule of the park.

Corrections

Email us with the page address and the agency link that shows the change. We update the verified date when we recheck.

Which FL Beach helps you pick a Florida beach from facts published by county and city parks, Florida State Parks, the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, and the Florida Department of Health. We do not post live water conditions. Read the managing agency page before you go.
