Atlantic Coast beaches

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Atlantic pages face east. Surf, inlets and sunrise light are the usual story, and red tide can still arrive by current.

Beaches

  • Anastasia State Park

    Anastasia is the St. Augustine state park with four miles of beach, a seasonal lifeguard window, a mat, and shelling the page describes.

  • North Peninsula State Park

    North Peninsula is the Atlantic park north of the Flagler line in this guide, with 2.5 miles of beach, shelling, surfing, and no lifeguard.

  • Little Talbot Island State Park

    Little Talbot is the Duval barrier island park with shelling, fossil shark teeth on the park's own list, two beach wheelchairs, and no lifeguards.

  • Sebastian Inlet State Park

    Sebastian Inlet is the Atlantic park with a protected cove the page recommends for children, plus surf, beachcombing, and no lifeguards.

  • Fort Pierce Inlet State Park

    Fort Pierce Inlet State Park is the St. Lucie beach with a firm bottom, shells, snorkeling, and a free wheelchair you request at the gate.

  • John D. MacArthur Beach State Park

    MacArthur Beach is the Palm Beach County state park with nearly two miles of Atlantic beach, three free wheelchairs, and shelling in the park's own list.

  • Hugh Taylor Birch State Park

    Hugh Taylor Birch is the Fort Lauderdale state park that opens onto the city beach, with 252 spaces and a beach wheelchair at the ranger station.

  • Crandon Park Beach

    Crandon Park is the Key Biscayne county beach with a published parking price, a lot under 3,000 spaces, and beach wheelchairs.

  • Bill Baggs Cape Florida State Park

    Bill Baggs is the Cape Florida park on Key Biscayne with swimming chairs, beach chairs, and a mat at access 6.
