Florida Keys beaches

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 16 Nov 2026

Keys pages use Florida State Parks. Some shores face the Atlantic, some face Florida Bay, and Bahia Honda has both.

Counties

  • Monroe County

Beaches

  • Loggerhead Beach at Bahia Honda

    Loggerhead Beach is the Bahia Honda shore the park page says faces the Atlantic, with shallow water.

  • Calusa Beach at Bahia Honda

    Calusa Beach is the Bahia Honda shore the park page says faces Florida Bay, with two covered picnic pavilions.

  • Sandspur Beach at Bahia Honda

    Sandspur Beach is the Bahia Honda shore with parking along the beach, facing the Atlantic, and a centralized restroom.

  • Fort Zachary Taylor Historic State Park

    Fort Zachary Taylor is the Key West state park the page calls the best swimming on the island, with snorkeling from shore and a rocky bottom.

  • Cannon Beach at John Pennekamp

    Cannon Beach is the Pennekamp shore the page calls the main snorkel beach, with shipwreck artifacts placed in shallow water.

  • Far Beach at John Pennekamp

    Far Beach is the other Pennekamp beach, which the page describes as a place to swim or sit under the palms rather than as the artifact snorkel spot.

  • Curry Hammock State Park

    Curry Hammock is the Marathon state park with a short Atlantic beach, beachcombing, and a lot that closes when it is full.

  • Long Key State Park

    Long Key State Park is the Atlantic shore with a beach wheelchair, shallow swimming, and a birding trail. The page does not describe shelling.
