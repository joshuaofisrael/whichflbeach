Florida beaches by region

Four coasts, one question: which beach fits the day you actually have.

  • Gulf Coast

    44 beaches. Sarasota, the islands north of it, Charlotte, Lee and Collier.

  • Atlantic Coast

    11 beaches. From Nassau and Duval down the east coast to Miami-Dade.

  • Panhandle

    12 beaches. Franklin, Bay, Walton, Okaloosa and Escambia state park beaches.

  • Florida Keys

    8 beaches. State park beaches from Key Largo to Key West.

Questions people ask

Which Florida coast should I open?

Open the Gulf Coast, the Atlantic Coast, the Panhandle, or the Florida Keys. Each region page lists its counties and beaches.

Is there one best beach in Florida?

No. Choose the constraint that would ruin the day, then open that beach page and the managing agency link.

Which FL Beach helps you pick a Florida beach from facts published by county and city parks, Florida State Parks, the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission, and the Florida Department of Health. We do not post live water conditions. Read the managing agency page before you go.
