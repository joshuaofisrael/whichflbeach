Gulf Coast beaches

By Which FL Beach · Last verified 20 Nov 2026

Gulf beaches from Pasco to Collier, chosen by parking, dogs, and lifeguards. Sunset direction is a separate guide.

This page is the county map for the Gulf shores in the guide. It is not a sunset ranking. Where the sun sets over the water answers the direction question and names the three park pages that mention sunset. The beaches below face the same water whether or not their park page says so.

Dog rules change at the county line. In this guide the Pinellas exception is the designated dog beach at Fort De Soto. Most Gulf state park pages we cite keep pets off the sand. Honeymoon Island is the state park with a named pet beach, separate from the main swim.

A lifeguard line is whatever that county prints. Siesta Beach has a published window on the Sarasota beaches page. Caspersen Beach is the opposite planning problem: on 20 Nov 2026 the county facility page said the Harbor Drive vehicle lot, the restrooms, and the playground were closed after the 2024 storm washout, with entry on foot or by bicycle from South Brohard. That fact is written out on the Caspersen page.

Boat-only islands are not a backup lot. Cayo Costa and Don Pedro are reached by boat. If the day depends on a car, pick a drive-up beach in the same county before you leave.

Beaches

  • Siesta Beach

    Siesta Beach is the Sarasota County park with the large free lot, a published lifeguard window, a free beach wheelchair and a 504 foot access mat.

  • Turtle Beach

    Turtle Beach is the staffed county park at the south end of Siesta Key, with a boat ramp, a fishing pier and a playground, and no lifeguard in the directory list.

  • Turtle Beach Campground

    Turtle Beach Campground is the county campground on Siesta Key. The directory title says it has no beach parking.

  • Lido Beach

    Lido Beach is the county beach on Lido Key with a lifeguard, a concession, a pool and restrooms on the directory list.

  • North Lido Beach

    North Lido Beach is the county access titled as having no parking. The directory lists beach access, birding, picnicking, an unpaved trail and wildlife viewing.

  • Ted Sperling Park at South Lido

    Ted Sperling Park at South Lido is the county park at the south end of Lido Key. The directory lists a playground, restrooms, picnicking and an unpaved trail, and it does not list swimming or a lifeguard.

  • Nokomis Beach

    Nokomis Beach is a guarded county beach with a playground, a concession, a fishing pier, a boat ramp and sand volleyball on the directory list.

  • North Jetty Park

    North Jetty Park is a guarded county beach at the south end of Casey Key, with a playground, a concession and a kayak launch on the directory list.

  • Venice Beach

    Venice Beach is a guarded county beach with a concession, sand volleyball and restrooms. The directory does not mention shark teeth or shelling.

  • Manasota Beach

    Manasota Beach is a guarded county beach on Manasota Key with boat ramps for motorized and paddle craft, fishing and restrooms.

  • Caspersen Beach

    Caspersen Beach is open on foot and by bicycle. The county says the vehicle lot, restrooms, and playground have been closed since the 2024 storm washout.

  • Blind Pass Beach

    Blind Pass Beach is a county park on Manasota Key with restrooms, swimming, fishing, a kayak launch and picnic shelters. A lifeguard is not in the list.

  • Palmer Point Beach

    Palmer Point is the county beach at the south end of Siesta Key that the directory limits to watercraft access. It lists swimming, fishing, a kayak launch and an unpaved trail.

  • Service Club Park

    Service Club Park is a county beach in Venice with restrooms, sand volleyball, grills and picnic shelters. A lifeguard is not in the list.

  • Maxine Barritt Park

    Maxine Barritt Park is one of two Sarasota County beach listings marked dog friendly with a leash required. It also lists swimming, fishing, restrooms and a paved trail.

  • North Brohard Park

    North Brohard Park is a Venice beach listing with a fishing pier, a bait shop, a concession and restrooms. A lifeguard is not in the list.

  • South Brohard Park

    South Brohard Park is a short county listing: beach access, birding, fishing, a restroom, swimming and wildlife viewing. No lifeguard is listed.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 1

    Siesta Key Beach Access 1 is the island access the county marks dog friendly with a leash required. The directory also lists swimming, birding and wildlife viewing, and it does not list a lifeguard or a restroom.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 3

    Siesta Key Beach Access 3 is one of the pedestrian accesses the county hours page says are open all hours and have no parking.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 3B

    Siesta Key Beach Access 3B is named with Access 3 on the county hours page as a 24 hour pedestrian access with no parking.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 4

    Siesta Key Beach Access 4 is a numbered neighborhood access. The directory gives it the short amenity list and does not list a lifeguard or a restroom.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 5

    Siesta Key Beach Access 5 is a separate county access with the short amenity list. It is not the main Siesta Beach pavilion.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 7

    Siesta Key Beach Access 7 is another numbered access with beach, birding, swimming, an unpaved trail and wildlife viewing on the directory list.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 8

    Siesta Key Beach Access 8 carries the short directory list and is not the lifeguard access.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 9

    Siesta Key Beach Access 9 is the last of the short list accesses before the pedestrian only points the hours page names.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 10

    Siesta Key Beach Access 10 is titled as pedestrian access with no parking. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 11

    Siesta Key Beach Access 11 is a no parking pedestrian access that still lists a lifeguard. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 12

    Siesta Key Beach Access 12 is a numbered access the directory does not mark as no parking. The amenity list is still short: beach, birding, swimming, an unpaved trail and wildlife viewing.

  • Siesta Key Beach Access 13

    Siesta Key Beach Access 13 is a no parking pedestrian access at the south end of the public list. The hours page includes it among the all hour pedestrian accesses.

  • Coquina Beach

    Coquina Beach is a Manatee County beach on Anna Maria with a lifeguard station, beach wheelchairs, a playground, and an introduction that talks about the sun setting over the water.

  • Manatee Public Beach

    Manatee Public Beach is the Holmes Beach county park with a published all year lifeguard schedule and beach wheelchairs on the amenity list.

  • Cortez Beach

    Cortez Beach is the third Manatee County beach the lifeguard page names. That page is specific about guards and dogs, and silent about the lot and the wheelchair.

  • Fort De Soto Park

    Fort De Soto is the Pinellas County park with miles of Gulf beach, two swim centers, and the only county dog beach.

  • Sand Key Park

    Sand Key is the Clearwater county beach with paid parking, a dog park that is not the beach, and seasonal lifeguards named in a county notice.

  • Honeymoon Island State Park

    Honeymoon Island is the Pinellas state park with a named pet beach, shelling on the north spit, and free beach wheelchairs.

  • Caladesi Island State Park

    Caladesi is the island park reached by ferry from Honeymoon Island. The page promotes shelling and bans live shells and pets on the beach.

  • Anclote Key Preserve State Park

    Anclote Key is a boat or ferry preserve in Pasco. Shells are mentioned, live shellfish are not allowed, and dogs are limited to North Anclote Bar.

  • Egmont Key State Park

    Egmont Key is a boat only island in Tampa Bay with shelling, no restrooms, and a south end the page closes as a shorebird refuge.

  • Stump Pass Beach State Park

    Stump Pass is the Charlotte County state park beach the page ties to winter shelling, with free wheelchairs and no lifeguards.

  • Don Pedro Island State Park

    Don Pedro is a boat only Gulf beach in Charlotte County. The drive up land base is not the swim.

  • Lovers Key State Park

    Lovers Key is the Lee County state park that calls its own beach some of the best shelling on that coast, and that warns the lot fills on busy weekends.

  • Gasparilla Island State Park

    Gasparilla Island State Park is the Boca Grande Gulf beach where the page highlights winter shelling and several named lots.

  • Cayo Costa State Park

    Cayo Costa is a boat access Lee County island the park page recommends for shells, with about nine miles of Gulf beach in the description.

  • Delnor Wiggins Pass State Park

    Delnor Wiggins is the Collier state park the page credits with some of the best Gulf shelling, four beach lots, and both manual and electric beach wheelchairs.

Questions people ask

Does every Gulf beach have the sun setting over the water?

The open water on this coast is to the west, so a clear evening can put the sun over the water. Weather, a dune, or a building can still block it. This page does not rank views. The sunset guide names the few park pages that actually mention sunset.

Which Gulf beach should I open first?

Open the constraint that would ruin the day. A dog points to Fort De Soto in this guide. A guarded swim points to the county pages that list lifeguards, such as Siesta Beach. A closed lot matters more than the coast: Caspersen's vehicle lot was closed on the county facility page checked 20 Nov 2026.
